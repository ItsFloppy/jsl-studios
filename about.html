About

The house behind the films

We make
cinema.

JSL Studios is an independent film studio built to bring the magic, scale and craft of cinema to a new generation of audiences.

Founded in New York, 2020.

Founded in New York in 2020, JSL began with independent feature films and has since grown into a multidisciplinary studio working across cinema, fashion, advertising and entertainment.

Our work ranges from original feature films and stories in development to high-end campaigns, fashion films, runway films, commercials and branded content.

Across every format, our ambition remains the same: to create work with the imagination, emotion and production value of cinema.

A studio. A production house. A creative platform. JSL operates across two complementary worlds.
JSL Studio

JSL STUDIO develops, write and produce our own films — building original stories, characters and worlds with the ambition to grow JSL into a distinctive international film studio. It is the creative engine behind the worlds, characters and stories we want to bring to audiences.

JSL Production

JSL PRODUCTION is where that same cinematic vision meets the world of brands, fashion and culture. We work with fashion houses, luxury brands, cultural partners and entertainment companies to create campaigns, commercials, fashion films, runway films, branded content and short-form productions designed with the scale and attention to detail of cinema.

Different projects. One vision.

From independent film to global studio

JSL was born independently, but our ambition has never been small.

Our first productions established the foundation. Each project has allowed us to grow our teams, our capabilities, our network and the scale of what we can create.

Today, JSL is building toward something larger: an international studio capable of developing original cinema, producing at scale and creating lasting relationships across the global entertainment and creative industries.

We are building a company designed to move between worlds — from the theatrical screen to fashion, from advertising to entertainment, from New York and Europe to new international markets.

Our vision

JSL Studios aims to become a global reference for independent, story-driven cinema — combining the creative freedom of an independent studio with the ambition, craft and production value traditionally associated with major studios.

We believe cinema can be spectacular without losing its soul, ambitious without losing its independence, and contemporary without losing the timeless magic that made audiences fall in love with movies in the first place. JSL draws from the golden age of Hollywood — its sense of spectacle, storytelling and movie magic — and reimagines it through a contemporary, international lens.

At the same time, we seek to push fashion, advertising and entertainment toward a more cinematic standard of visual storytelling.

Our work is ultimately about one thing: creating moments of joy, entertainment, escapism and movie magic for audiences around the world.

New York—Paris—The world

Born between France and the United States, JSL has always been international by nature. Today, our network is expanding into new territories, including Saudi Arabia, where we are building new relationships and creative opportunities across film, fashion and entertainment.

We believe the future of cinema and creative production is increasingly global — and JSL intends to be part of that future.

Justin Souriau-Levine

Our CEO

Justin Souriau-Levine

Justin Souriau-Levine is an award-winning director, producer, writer and actor, and the Founder & CEO of JSL Studios.

Since founding the studio in New York in 2020, he has led JSL across independent feature films, commercial production, fashion and branded content.

A three-time New York Emmy recipient as Executive Producer, Justin brings a background in both filmmaking and performance to his work. Before cinema, he performed with American Ballet Theatre at major venues including the Metropolitan Opera House, Lincoln Center and the Kennedy Center.

Today, he is building JSL with a broader ambition: to grow an international film studio where original cinema, creative production and global collaboration come together.
