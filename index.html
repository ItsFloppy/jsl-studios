Who we are
JSL Studios is a New York City, Paris and Riyadh based independent film and commercial production company which produces narrative feature films, commercials and branded content.
Since our inception in 2020, JSL has been a harbor for joy, entertainment, and escapism. From our feature films to our commercial and fashion productions, every project is part of a larger journey: to spread joy, imagination, and entertainment across the world through the magic of cinema.

Joy is why we started. Craft is how we keep it.
